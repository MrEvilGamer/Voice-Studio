# Voice Studio identity

The emblem is an original VS monogram with angular orange metal, ember-red faces and a restrained flame/signal stroke. It follows the supplied Flare reference's fiery visual direction while using Voice Studio's initials.

`voice-studio-emblem.png` is the transparent full-resolution master, created with the built-in imagegen tool. `voice-studio-logo.png` is the normalized UI mark. Tauri PNG and seven-resolution Windows ICO exports are generated with `python scripts/build-icons.py` (Pillow required). `scripts/make-icons.ps1` and `scripts/make-ico.cjs` delegate to that same packager.

The emblem appears in the compact sidebar, expanded wordmark, header identity, browser favicon and native window/tray/installer icons. Font files are bundled locally under `public/fonts` with their OFL licenses.

## Generation prompt

Create one production-ready logo emblem for the Windows audio software Voice Studio. Use case: logo-brand. A clearly readable, distinctive interlocked V and S monogram, angular esports emblem with sharp diagonal cuts, compact square overall silhouette. The V is strong on the left and the S legible on the right, connected as one coherent custom emblem. Inspired by the warm fiery orange/red metallic energy of the Flare emblem visible in the conversation, but an ORIGINAL VS identity, never an F. Stylized clean beveled orange metal, bright amber edges and deep ember-red inner faces, restrained flame ribbons integrated into the upper left stroke (not surrounding decorative fire). Convey flowing sound/energy through the geometry. Strong bold silhouette and generous negative spaces so the VS remains readable at small app icon size. Centered emblem with roughly 10 percent transparent padding, fully transparent background with genuine alpha. No dark background, no badge container, no wordmark, no extra letters, no tiny sparks, no mockups or presentation board, no watermark. Front-facing balanced vector-friendly illustrated emblem, subtle dimensional highlights rather than photorealism.
